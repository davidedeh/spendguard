# References

Public policies used as models for the structure, tone and level of detail of the fictional Halden Group policy. No text is copied from them.

- [HM Treasury Group travel and expenses policy (June 2013)](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/224100/hmt_group_travel_and_subsistence_policy.pdf): travel, subsistence, entertainment, hospitality and gifts. May have been superseded; used for structure only.
- [King's College London expenses rules](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/assets/policyzone/finance/expenses-rules.pdf): the "wholly, exclusively and necessarily" test, meal limits, accommodation.
- [University of Waikato Purchasing and Sensitive Expenditure Policy](https://www.waikato.ac.nz/about/governance/docs/policies/purchasing-policy): purchasing channels, purchase orders, sensitive expenditure.
